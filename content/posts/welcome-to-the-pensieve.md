I've always wanted a small, slow corner of the internet — somewhere to jot down what I've been reading, what surprised me in a paper, or how I'd explain a difficult concept to a student over coffee.

So here it is. **The Pensieve** is a handpicked notebook, not a feed. Posts will land when they're ready, not on a schedule. Expect three kinds of writing:

## What you'll find here

- **Research notes** — quick reactions to papers I'm reading on agentic AI, federated learning, and adversarial defense.
- **Tutorials** — practical walk-throughs (PyTorch, Spark, prompt engineering) aimed at students who already know the basics but want depth.
- **Field reflections** — short, honest takes on what's working and what isn't in AI security research.

## What you won't find here

- Hot takes on the latest model release. There are enough of those already.
- Recycled survey-style overviews. If I can't add something concrete, I won't post.

If you stumble across something useful, send me a note. The best part of writing publicly is the conversations it starts.

— *Naila*
